Epilogue · Reading Fog Decades

Switch to the Oakland tab

On Fog Decades, look at SFO’s grid of summers, then press Oakland. The grid gets darker, almost every year, and darkest in early August.

Data: the NOAA hourly airport observations Fog Decades ships. “Low cloud” is a reading with the cloud ceiling at or below , on the eight 3-hourly readings of a day, as on the main page; a day has low cloud if any of the eight did. Oakland has no record for 2000–03, so those summers are left out of every comparison. Two airports are not the Bay, and two instruments are not one: some of the gap could be how each airport reports a ceiling. This says where the low cloud was recorded, not why.
